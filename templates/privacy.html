Your privacy

Huey asks for a photo of your face, so we want to be clear about exactly what happens to it, and to everything else you share with us.

Last updated 19 October 2026

Your photo is never stored

When you upload a photo or take a selfie, your image is sent securely to our server, analyzed in memory, and discarded as soon as the result is returned. It is not saved, logged or shared, and it is never used to train anything. We don't keep the skin colour measured from it either, only the Monk Skin Tone you choose to save.

What we keep

If you create an account:

  • Your username, email address, and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your actual password.
  • Your chosen Monk Skin Tone and, if it came from a photo, how clear the match was.
  • Your answer to how reactive your skin is to the sun.

Only if you choose to send them:

  • Contact form messages: your name, email address, message and, for brands, your company and website, so we can reply.
  • Questionnaire answers: every question is optional, including your age range, how you describe your skin tone and your email address. Answers are only looked at in total and are never shown to anyone. If you leave your email, we use it only to invite you to try Huey early.

If you're signed in when you send a message or answer the questionnaire, it's linked to your account.

How we use it

We use your tone and sensitivity only to show you products that match them. Your email is used to sign you in, to reply when you contact us, and, if you asked for it in the questionnaire, to invite you to try Huey early. We don't sell any of it or share it with advertisers.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you signed in. It's removed when you close your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Other services

Huey's page styling is loaded from jsDelivr, a public content network, which sees your IP address when your browser fetches it. Like most websites, the servers that host Huey may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. Retailer links open the retailer's own site, where their privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use Delete account on your profile page to permanently delete your account, your skin profile and any messages or questionnaire answers linked to it. To see what we hold about you, correct it, or delete something you sent without an account, email us at ackeem@tryhuey.com.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions: ackeem@tryhuey.com.
